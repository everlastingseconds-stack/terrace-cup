Rules / How to Play

The draft. Deploy field units drawn at random from across the animal kingdom — when it is your turn, receive your next defender, complete with attack, defence, speed, and power ratings from central records.

The fortress runs a single-elimination knockout tournament seeded at random from however many commanders are playing — byes are handed out automatically if the field isn't a clean power of two. Every match in the current round runs concurrently — only one is "live" on screen at a time, but every knockout hands the arena straight to another still-unresolved match in that round, so no commander sits waiting through an entire match before getting a turn. Each match is a running skirmish — commanders keep locking in fresh combatants, duel after duel, until one side's entire garrison is down; the survivor advances to the next round. Only the opening duel of each match is sealed on both sides — both commanders commit privately and the picks are revealed together. From the second duel onward, whichever commander won the previous duel gets to see their opponent's next pick before locking in their own — a running edge for staying on top. A double knockout resets that edge: the following duel is sealed for both sides again, just like the opening duel. Once engaged, a duel plays out round by round: each round both commanders privately pick a stance — Aggressive (+damage dealt, +damage taken), Balanced, or Defensive (-damage dealt, -damage taken) — sealed until both commit. Either side may instead call a Retreat, pulling their fighter out with its current HP and Stamina intact rather than risking a knockout, at the cost of extra Stamina and a limited number of uses per match. Each unit carries two service meters: HP (health, permanent) and Stamina (energy, drains from fighting). Only bench units belonging to the two commanders currently fighting recover +20 stamina (capped at 100) each round; commanders not in this match are unaffected. A unit reduced to zero HP is declared down and takes no further part in the match. Every garrison is built in a fixed draft order, each step a 3-card choice: first two Apex-rank units, then three Veteran/Recruit-rank units — guaranteed at least one of each — and finally two Elite-rank units, filling all seven roster slots. Terrain (selectable up top) applies a live stat modifier to every unit in every fight: on their home terrain, matching animals get +10% to all stats, while everyone else takes -5%. "World" runs with no terrain modifier at all. Terrain also tilts the draft: animals native to the chosen terrain are 4x as likely to appear among your 3 offered cards (in every phase, rerolls included), though the draft order and rank rules stay the same. With exactly 3 commanders the game runs a Tri-Series instead: everyone plays everyone, one match at a time (A v B, then B v C, then A v C), each played to the end with all garrisons restored to full between matches. The top 2 on wins reach the Final; if all three win one match each, the most garrison HP remaining at the end of their win is the tie-breaker. The Final is played exactly like any other Final.

Game modes. The host picks a mode when creating the room. Classic uses the rules above. Blitzkrieg drafts only Apex and Elite animals — every garrison is exactly 3 Apex, then 4 Elite (up to 8 commanders). Endurance drafts only Veteran and Recruit animals — every garrison is exactly 3 Veterans and 4 Recruits. In Opponent's Oppression your opponent chooses your draft picks: each time you draft, the 3 offered animals go to them and they decide which one you get (your opponent is your first-round bracket opponent; with a bye or in a 3-player series it is the next seat around the table). In Knockout King each garrison is 10 animals — 3 Apex, 3 Elite, 2 Veterans, 2 Recruits — and a match is won by the first commander to knock out 5 enemy animals; the live knockout score is shown in bold. If both commanders reach 5 in the same duel, it is sudden death: the next knockout wins.
